About this replay

Everything shown is what Passive Monitor had recorded at each moment — nothing is back-dated or inferred. Road disruptions are limited to weather-related causes () that started during the event. Flood graphs are shown for every gauge that reached Minor or above during the event, grouped by river basin. Trend lines are observations, not forecasts.

Keys: Space play/pause · ←/→ 15 min · Shift+arrows 1 hour · Home/End. Click a gauge on the map to jump to its graph; click a graph or a timeline entry to jump the clock.
